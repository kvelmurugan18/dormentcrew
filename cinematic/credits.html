Sources & credits

ECHOES — The Last Transmission is an independent interactive film by Dorment Crew. The 2026 expedition featuring Yuva and E.D.I.T.H. is dramatized. The equipment histories, signal losses and repair accounts are based on mission records.

Mission records

The equipment archive links to the NASA and JPL sources for each record. The historical repair scenes draw on the Apollo 17 mission report and JPL’s report on Spirit’s 2004 recovery. The return scenes draw on the Voyager one-light-day announcement and the Golden Record overview.

Film and exploration

  • The full-screen chapter transitions take inspiration from A Solar Tale.
  • The fictional mission uses historical launch footage from NASA’s Artemis II launch, Artemis II training footage, and NASA Orion animation as visual references. The Mars briefing includes NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS dust-storm footage.
  • Apollo 12 descent-stage animation by Chris Smith, NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio. Apollo 17 flyover by Ernie Wright, NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio.
  • Voyager and Golden Record motion by NASA / Jonathan North. The Moon’s night sky uses a 4096 × 2048 adaptation of the ESO / S. Brunier Milky Way panorama under CC BY 4.0. The rendered Mars sky draws on NASA’s description of Martian daylight.
  • Earth imagery by Reto Stöckli and Robert Simmon from NASA Goddard’s Blue Marble. Suit model by DigitalSpace Corporation from NASA 3D Resources, adapted for movement.
  • The Spirit and Opportunity model comes from NASA’s Visualization Technology Applications and Development team.
  • Intrepid’s descent stage adapts NASA and Michael D. Carbajal’s lunar module model.
  • Yuva’s close-ups incorporate edited stock performances from Mikhail Nilov’s cockpit clip and helmet clip under the Pexels license.
  • The Moon terrain adapts the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera Apollo 12 terrain model for a compact walking route. Ground texture by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven under CC0. Equipment views credit their mission imagery individually.
  • Neil Armstrong photographed Buzz Aldrin for the menu’s Apollo 11 image. Original score and female English voices were generated locally using Kokoro. Codex assisted with writing, implementation, animation and tests.
  • 3D rendering with Three.js r160, Draco mesh decoding from Google Draco, and offline encoding with FFmpeg.
