Credits

ECHOES: The Last Transmission is a fictional local game adapted from the supplied ECHOES project pack. The AURORA story, future dialogue, routes and relay operations are dramatized. The astronaut, rover and spacecraft scenes are stylized reconstructions.

  • Earth globe land mask adapted from Natural Earth’s public-domain map data. The atmosphere, terrain, planet textures and equipment models were drawn procedurally.
  • The Voyager starscape uses The Milky Way panorama · ESO/S. Brunier, under CC BY 4.0. It is rotated and dimmed as a 3D sky texture.
  • Rendering with Three.js r160 under the MIT license. The opening was rendered from the same scenes and encoded locally with FFmpeg.
  • Original score and English dialogue, generated locally. Character voices use Windows speech synthesis. ECHO-7 is a fictional AI character with scripted lines and actions.
  • The Voyager sequence draws on NASA’s Voyager mission history and Golden Record overview. The film uses no NASA photographs, video, recordings or live feeds.

Codex assisted with the implementation, procedural visuals, soundtrack and tests. This project is independent and has no affiliation with NASA.
